# Third-Party Notices

The repository as a whole is released under the MIT License (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
The skills listed below are third-party works that are **not** covered by that
license: each one is distributed under the terms stated in its own folder, and
those terms apply to that folder only.

Provenance was checked by comparing each `SKILL.md` body with the upstream
repository (frontmatter ignored). "Verified" means the body is identical to the
named upstream revision; "not verified" means no upstream license could be
established, and nothing was added.

The catalogue adds frontmatter fields (`id`, `source`, `category`, ...) to the
skill files it redistributes; skill bodies are unchanged. Only `SKILL.md` and the
license file are shipped here, not the upstream helper files (scripts, references).
